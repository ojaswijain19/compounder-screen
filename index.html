How the score works

Every metric is turned into a percentile (0–100). By default each stock is ranked against its own sector, so a bank is compared with banks and a chipmaker with chipmakers. Sectors with fewer than 12 stocks reporting a metric fall back to the whole universe. Metrics are averaged into six factors, then weighted by your sliders.

Growth
TTM revenue growth, 3- and 5-year revenue CAGR, and how many of the last 5 years revenue grew.
Durability
5-year average ROIC (ROE for banks and insurers), FCF margin after stock comp, operating margin, and how stable that margin has been.
Trajectory
Operating-margin change over 3 years, growth acceleration (TTM growth vs 3-year CAGR), and share count change. Buybacks score well; dilution scores badly.
Value
FCF yield after stock comp, earnings yield, and where today's price-to-sales sits within the stock's own last 5 years.
Balance sheet
Net debt ÷ EBITDA. Net cash ranks best.
Trend
6- and 12-month price return and distance from the 40-week (≈200-day) average.
Cycle-peak penalty
Stocks with a cyclical history (a revenue drop of 10% or more in the last 6 years, or volatile margins) lose 8 points when today's margin sits far above their 5-year average or revenue is up over 50% in a year. Peak earnings flatter every ratio, and this corrects for that.

Read before you act

  • Figures are as-reported GAAP/IFRS from 10-K and 10-Q XBRL, trailing twelve months, with no adjustment for one-offs, write-downs or investment gains.
  • Foreign filers (20-F) report annually. Non-USD reporters show no valuation metrics.
  • "Stale" means the latest SEC period ended more than 200 days ago, usually because of a tagging change or annual-only filing. Stale stocks are hidden by default.
  • ≈ marks an approximation: pre-tax income where no operating income is tagged, or net debt ÷ EBIT where D&A isn't tagged.
  • Insurers' and some financials' cash flow includes float, so their FCF can overstate owner earnings.
  • This is a starting list for research, not a recommendation. Read the filings and the call, and check the thesis, before acting on any name.
